# Zorge 101 plan complaint — 2026-08-31

## Complaint reproduced

The complaint refers to bakery 23 (`Зорге 101 Казань`) and the plan for
2026-08-31 generated by run
`prod_base_bakery_norm_recent_20260830_h14`.

| SKU | Historical daily mean | Historical Monday mean | Raw forecast | Displayed plan |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 108 Сметанник | 20.7 | 17.6 | 0.745 | 1 |
| 1071 Треугольник курица безд | 198.4 | 215.1 | 418.447 | 420 |

The plan renderer only exposes the forecast error: 0.745 is rounded to one
unit and 418.447 is rounded to the baking multiple. Previous-day stock is not
the root cause.

## Decomposition

The bakery total is moderately low rather than extreme: forecast 1,424.5
against a recent historical Monday mean around 1,649.9.

The main error is allocation:

| Category | Historical Monday mean | Current forecast |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Пироги сладкие | 53.6 | 4.0 |
| Выпечка сытная | 863.7 | 1,213.5 |

Within savory bakery, SKU 1071 also receives about 34.5% of the forecast
category, versus about 24.9% of historical Monday savory sales. Category mass
inflation and within-category SKU inflation multiply, producing the 418-unit
forecast.

For SKU 108, the sweet-pie category collapses by more than 13x and its
within-category share is also low, producing the near-zero forecast.

## Relation to the selected Direct candidate

The closest frozen-fold Monday case is bakery 23 on 2026-08-17:

| SKU | Actual | Current | Direct P50 | Selected alpha 0.25 |

The Direct candidate fixes the same failure mode because it allocates the
bakery-day volume directly across SKU. It does not preserve the erroneous
hour-derived category total, and it does not normalize a sparse hourly SKU
profile inside that category.

## Conclusion

This is not a new independent defect. It is a current production example of
the inherited hourly/category allocation problem that motivated the Direct
candidate. The 2026-08-28 assortment-mass hotfix prevented filtered bakeable
rows from absorbing the entire bakery forecast, but it did not replace the
hour-derived relative distribution among surviving categories and SKU.

Production was queried read-only and was not changed.
